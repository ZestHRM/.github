# ZestNexus 🚀
### The AI-Powered Business Operating Platform

![ZestNexus Logo](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ad35f06a-4823-4a7e-923b-142cc29306d4)

ZestNexus is an all-in-one business operating platform that helps organizations manage their workforce, customers, projects, and daily operations from a single place.

Built for startups, SMBs, and growing enterprises, ZestNexus combines **HRMS**, **Workforce Management**, **Recruitment**, **Project Management**, **CRM**, **Payroll**, and **AI-powered automation** to simplify business operations and improve team productivity.
